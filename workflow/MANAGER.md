# DS Create Builder

DS Create Builder is the local app used during init. Its documentation is split into four files.

- [builder/lifecycle.md](builder/lifecycle.md): starting Builder, identity and progress, what Builder shows, the handoff from init, saving and undo, sync state, and motion.
- [builder/publish.md](builder/publish.md): the Publish panel, Figma generation, MCP delivery and recovery.
- [builder/parts.md](builder/parts.md): Parts in Builder.
- [builder/setup.md](builder/setup.md): web-first setup, source revisions and the foundation plan.

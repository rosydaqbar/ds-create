# 3.8 Badge group

A Badge group pairs a compact Badge with a short message, for announcements, release notes and update notices above a page or section title. The whole group is one link: it points to the thing the message announces.

Use a Badge group when a short label ("New", "Beta", "v2.4") needs a one-line message next to it that links somewhere. A label on its own is the Badge Part (2.4), which also holds the tone, type and content documentation. A message that needs a title, body or actions is a banner or alert, not a Badge group.

# 4. Badge group

## Properties

Variant name pattern: `Size=md, Placement=left, Type=pill, Tone=brand, State=rest`

| Property | Type | Values | Default | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Size` | variant | `md, lg` | `md` | heights, inner Badge size and type |
| `Placement` | variant | `left, right` | `left` | the badge sits before or after the message |
| `Type` | variant | `pill, outline` | `pill` | group container treatment: tinted fill, or neutral surface with a border |
| `Tone` | variant | `neutral, brand, danger, warning, success` | `brand` | colours of the container and the badge |
| `State` | variant | `rest, hover, focus` | `rest` | the group is one link: hover deepens the container, focus adds the ring |
| `Badge label` | text | — | "New feature" | label of the inner Badge (exposed nested instance) |
| `Message` | text | — | "We've just released a new update" | the message |
| `Show trailing icon` | boolean | — | `true` | arrow after the message |
| `Trailing icon` | instance swap | icons | `arrow-right` | — |

Variants: 2 sizes × 2 placements × 2 types × 5 tones × 3 states = **120**.

The Badge stays a real instance; never recreate its label or style inline. Changing the Badge Part changes every Badge group.

| Size | Inner Badge | Message style | Padding, badge side | Padding, message side | Trailing icon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `md` | Badge `Size=sm` | `type/body/sm/medium` | `badge-group/padding-badge/md` = 4 | `badge-group/padding-message/md` = 10 | `size/icon/sm` = 16 |
| `lg` | Badge `Size=md` | `type/body/sm/medium` | `badge-group/padding-badge/lg` = 4 | `badge-group/padding-message/lg` = 10 | `size/icon/sm` = 16 |

The badge-side padding is tighter so the inner badge sits optically centred against the rounded end of the group.

# 5. States

| State | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| `rest` | container fill and border from the tone |
| `hover` | container fill moves to its hover child (`color/fill/{tone}/subtle/hover`; outline: `color/surface/base/hover`) |
| `focus` | `focus/default` ring around the whole group (`Tone=danger`: `focus/danger`) |

The inner Badge has no states; only the group reacts.

# 7. Content

- The badge is one or two words ("New", "Beta", "v2.4"); the message is one short sentence of up to about eight words, with no full stop.
- The message says what happened or what is new, not "Click here".
- The trailing arrow is on whenever the group links somewhere.

# 8. Guidelines

## 8.1 Badge group, Badge or banner
Visual: three rows — a Badge "Beta" next to a feature name, a Badge group "New · Dark mode is here" above a page title, and a full-width banner with a title, body and a button. Rule: a Badge labels; a Badge group announces in one line and links; a banner explains and asks for action.

## 8.2 Anatomy: the Badge inside
Show the anatomy with the nested Badge highlighted, captioned "Badge group reuses the real Badge."

## 8.3 Placement and type
- `Placement=left` is the default; use `right` when the message is the main point and the badge only qualifies it ("This feature may change · Beta").
- `pill` for announcements on plain pages; `outline` on busy or tinted backgrounds where a tinted container would be noisy.
- Visual: the same group in `left` and `right`, and in `pill` and `outline`, on a plain and a tinted background.

## 8.4 Choosing a tone
- `brand` for new features and announcements, `success` for completed releases, `warning` for beta or changing features, `danger` for incidents, `neutral` for general notices.
- The badge label and message always name the meaning; colour supports it.

## 8.5 Accessibility
- The group is one link with one accessible name made of the badge label and the message ("New: Dark mode is here").
- The focus ring wraps the whole group, never just the badge.
- Message text meets text contrast against the container in every tone and mode.

## 8.6 Do / don't
- Do: "New · Dark mode is here →" above a page title. Don't: two Badge groups stacked above the same title.
- Don't: a Badge group with a two-line message; use a banner.
- Don't: a Badge group whose badge is drawn by hand instead of a Badge instance.

## 8.7 Maintenance
Change the badge look in 2.4 Badge; change the container through the tone tokens. Never edit individual variants.

# 9. QA

Fail QA when:
- the badge is recreated instead of instancing `Badge` (2.4);
- the inner Badge does not follow the group's `Size`, `Tone` and opposite `Type`;
- `Placement=right` does not move the Badge after the message;
- hover or focus applies to the inner Badge instead of the group;
- the message wraps to two lines;
- the set is published on the 2.4 Badge page instead of this page.
